About the farm

A working farm, not a brand built around one

FehmYanga Enterprise is a family-run agribusiness on the Kakamega–Webuye Road in Malava. We grow sugarcane, maize and vegetables on land we know well, using practices that keep the soil productive season after season — crop rotation, minimal chemical input, and harvesting only when produce is ready, not when it's convenient.

What leaves our farm gate is what came out of our fields that week. No middlemen holding stock, no guessing how old it is.
